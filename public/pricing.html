Frequently asked questions

Which currency are the prices in?

All prices are in Bangladeshi Taka (৳ BDT).

Which type of plan should I choose?

Local Business is for shops and services that take appointments, like cafés, restaurants, plumbers, electricians and clinics. E-commerce is for selling products online. Course Selling is for creators, influencers and YouTubers selling courses.

What do I get with a yearly plan?

You pay for 12 months at once. On Business and Pro plans, the yearly plan also comes with a free custom domain. The Local Business Pro plan includes a free domain for 1 year even when you pay monthly.

Can I use my own domain on Starter?

Yes. Every plan, including Starter, lets you connect a custom domain to your website.

What is CMS access?

The CMS is your dashboard for editing pages, adding products and updating your website — no coding needed. It's free on every plan.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can move to a bigger or smaller plan, or to a different type of plan, whenever your business needs change.

Is support really free?

Yes. Every plan includes free support to help you set up and run your website.
