Frequently asked questions

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes — ৳0, forever, with no card needed. You get a 5 page website, 3 products and your own address at yourstore.shopilier.com. Upgrade whenever your business is ready.

Which currency are the prices in?

All prices are in Bangladeshi Taka (৳ BDT).

What do I get with a yearly plan?

You pay for 12 months at once. On Business and Pro plans, the yearly plan also comes with a free custom domain.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, on Starter, Business and Pro you can connect a domain like www.yourbusiness.com. The Free plan uses your free shopilier.com address.

What is CMS access?

The CMS is your dashboard for editing pages, adding products and updating your website — no coding needed. It's free on every plan.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can move to a bigger or smaller plan whenever your business needs change — your website and products stay as they are.

How does support work?

Starter, Business and Pro include free support: message the Shopilier team from the chat button in your dashboard. The Free plan has no support, but the Help Center is open to everyone.
