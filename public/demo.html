This is the real Shopilier dashboard running on a sample store, Dhaka Threads. Add a product, record an order, customize the website and publish it — nothing to install, no sign-up.

Everything you change in the demo stays in this browser only. Use Reset demo in the green bar to start over.

Things to try

1

Add a product

Open Products → Add product. Give it a name, price, photo and sizes or colours.

2

Record an order

Open Orders → New order, the way you'd note a Facebook or phone order. Stock updates by itself.

3

Customize the website

Open Online Store → Themes → Customize. Click any part of the page to edit it, then publish.

4

Change the plan

Open Settings → Plan & billing and switch between Free, Starter, Business and Pro to see what each unlocks.
